## Rules

Each match consists of a best of 3 series of games.

### Army Composition

1. Each player must compose a army list not exceeding 500 points, with one character specified as Warlord.

2. No unit may contain a model with toughness 10 or higher at the time of Declare Battle Formations.

3. Each list must contain two units of non-character Infantry.

4. No epic heroes. No Legends units. No special characters (Emperor's Champion, The Sanguinor, etc).

5. You may not take 2 of the same unit, unless that unit is Battleline, in which case you may take no more than 2 of that unit.

6. A list must be made available for public viewing on this website before being used.

7. Players are entitled to a single list change during the season. Notify Rhys if you change your list. Lists may only be changed prior to the semi finals.

### Logistics

1. Players will play on the Play-on-Tabletop Colosseum board.

2. Each match will be a best-of-3 game series

3. Deployment Zones extend 9 inches from a player's side of the field. A player's Terrirory extends 18" from a player's side of the field.

4. For all rules purposes, a player's Expansion Objective is the rightmost objective in No Mans Land when viewed from that player's side of the table.

5. Players may use up to 3 Detachment Points and will use the Forced Dispositions of their armies as laid out in the Core Rules. Note that this means you can use both a 2DP and a 1DP detachment despite being a 500 point game.

6. Players roll off to determine Attacker and Defender, with the Attacker being able to choose the Mission Twist from the listed below:
RUINSCAPE
MARTIAL PRIDE
SCRAMBLED COMMUNICATIONS

7. From game 2 onwards, the loser of the previous game decides to be Attacker or Defender.

8. INFILTRATE units may not be deployed touching more than 1 Objective outside of their Deployment Zone.


### Terrain Rules

1. All terrain pieces will be considered Solid/Dense Terrain as laid out in the Core Rules.

2. All Objective Areas are considered to be Exposed Terrain as laid out in the Core Rules.

3. The outer Colosseum Walls are considered impassable. INFANTRY, BEAST, and SWARM units may traverse the Colosseum Walls by paying a 4” movement tax. Flying units must Take to the Skies to traverse the Colosseum Walls.

4. The outer Colosseum Walls may be traversed in this fashion during a Normal, Advance, Fall-Back, or Charge move.

5. Units disembarking from transports may not be set up on the opposite side of the Colosseum Walls from the transport they are disembarking from.

6. The Colosseum Walls are considered to be 2.01” thick for the purposes of determining Engagement Range. Units on opposite sides of the wall are not considered to be engagement range of one another.


### Scoring

1. Players play primary mission as determined by their force dispositions

### Secondaries FAQ

- The "Plunder" secondary can be scored by completing the action on your opponent's natural expansion even though it is in your territory.
